Netlife: Hong Kong

A pixel-art life sim set on Tung Tau Wan Road, Stanley — where the job is the lesson. You take networking work up and down the road, get paid, get burned, wire your own flat, and if you get careless with someone else's network, you end up at number 99. Played in free time, in a browser, on a laptop or an iPad.

One focus surface

Eleven bordered panels became two. Edges belong to physical objects — paper, a laptop, a sign.

Three numbers

Time, money, energy. Everything else is a menu or a prompt, or it gets deleted.

No OS keyboard

Steppers and an in-game keypad, because an iPad keyboard covers half the screen.

Getting it wrong

Evidence, not dice

Nothing is random. Careless players generate evidence; careful ones leave a trail that clears them.

Real law

Crimes Ordinance s.161, PDPO, Copyright Ordinance — cited on the sheet so the teacher can check it.

Boring on purpose

Procedural, dull, expensive. Punishment is not the fun part; the fix is.
